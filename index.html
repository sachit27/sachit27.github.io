I am a Senior Scientist and Lecturer in the Computational Social Science (COSS) group at ETH Zurich.

My research operates at the intersection of environmental geography, spatial analysis, and computational social science. I study how communities and cities adapt to environmental changes, combining nature-based solutions (NBS) with technological infrastructures to foster climate resilience.

Specifically, I develop computational frameworks and sensing methodologies to understand and enhance urban environments. This work spans three interconnected layers:

I build participatory environmental monitoring systems (like AirBox and Soc-IoT) that empower citizens to gather high-resolution climate data. Using spatial analysis and machine learning, I quantify socio-spatial inequities in urban resilience and green infrastructure (such as the award-winning greenR package). Additionally, I explore AI for the Environment and Value-Sensitive Design (VSD) to understand how AI can be responsibly leveraged to facilitate citizen feedback and integrate diverse knowledge systems in urban planning and climate adaptation.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Cambridge and a Research Fellow at the University of Surrey. I hold a Ph.D. in Social Networks & Human-Centered Computing from Academia Sinica and National Chengchi University.

Research Program

Urban Resilience, Nature-Based Solutions, and Environmental AI

My research investigates how to design robust, adaptive, and inclusive urban ecosystems. I use agent-based modelling, spatial analysis, and machine learning to understand how environmental technologies and infrastructure solutions spread across communities.

Urban Resilience & Nature-Based Solutions (NBS)

Developing spatial analysis frameworks and suitability mapping tools to evaluate urban green infrastructure. A major focus is moving beyond averages to reveal socio-spatial inequities in urban greenery and mapping active mobility patterns to inform climate adaptation.

greenR Climate Adaptation Urban Greenery

Participatory Environmental Monitoring

Building modular sensing infrastructure (Soc-IoT, AirBox, AirKit) and dynamic trust-based calibration algorithms for large-scale distributed urban sensing. Empowering citizens to collect air, water, and noise quality data for community-led environmental governance.

Citizen Science Sensor Networks Data Calibration

AI for Environment & Value Sensitive Design

Leveraging AI and computational models to gather citizen feedback, moderate multi-actor stakeholder engagements, and quantify value conflicts. Using responsible technology design (VSD) to ensure environmental interventions are inclusive and democratically grounded.

Agent-Based Modelling AI Moderation VSD

Vision: By balancing environmental analytics, nature-based interventions, citizen participation, and responsible AI, we can architect climate-resilient cities that are both technologically advanced and socially equitable.
